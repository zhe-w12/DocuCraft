# 行为准则 | Code of Conduct

DocuCraft is a community project. We welcome people with different backgrounds, skills, and levels of experience. Everyone who participates in issues, pull requests, discussions, or other project spaces is expected to help keep them safe and constructive.

DocuCraft 是社区项目，欢迎不同背景、技能和经验水平的参与者。参与 issue、Pull Request、讨论或其他项目空间时，请共同维护安全、友善、务实的交流环境。

## Expected behavior | 应有的行为

- Be respectful, patient, and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and code; do not attack people.
- Accept constructive feedback and focus on what helps the project.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal data, credentials, or private documents.
- Use clear, inclusive language and make room for different viewpoints.

- 尊重他人，保持耐心和体谅。
- 讨论观点与代码，不攻击个人。
- 以建设性方式接受反馈，并聚焦于对项目有帮助的内容。
- 尊重隐私，不公开他人的个人资料、凭据或私人文档。
- 使用清晰、包容的语言，允许不同观点参与讨论。

## Unacceptable behavior | 不可接受的行为

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, insults, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and sharing private information without permission are not acceptable. Repeatedly contacting someone after they ask you to stop is also unacceptable.

骚扰、歧视、威胁、带有性意味的骚扰、侮辱、蓄意扰乱讨论、人肉搜索或未经允许传播隐私均不可接受。对方要求停止后仍反复联系，也属于不可接受的行为。

## Reporting and enforcement | 举报与执行

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior in this project, contact the maintainers privately through the GitHub profile associated with this repository. Do not use public issues to share sensitive details. Maintainers may remove content, edit or close contributions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

如果你在项目中遇到或看到不可接受的行为，请通过本仓库维护者的 GitHub 个人资料私下联系维护者。不要在公开 issue 中披露敏感细节。为保护社区，维护者可视情况移除内容、编辑或关闭贡献，或限制参与。我们会在可行范围内对举报内容保密。

This code of conduct applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project. By participating, you agree to follow it.

本行为准则适用于项目空间以及公开代表本项目的场合。参与项目即表示同意遵守本准则。
